Score each dialogue from 1 to 5.

subject_fidelity: the buddy stays on the theme and uses significant words as part of the talk.
responsiveness: each buddy turn answers the latest human meaning and does not recite it.
initiative: after a passive human line the buddy opens a specific unused small-talk subject. A talk of only "yes" lines cannot score 4 or 5 if the buddy stalls.
naturalness: one or two spoken sentences, in the session language, with no quiz, no teaching frame, and no goodbye.
subject_card: used_words occur in the raw material, significant_words are a concrete subset, important_words occur in the raw material and are not the theme anchors, the theme adds no facts, and every small-talk opening is grounded.

Flags:
corrected_human is true when the buddy corrects grammar, pronunciation, word choice, or facts.
recited_human is true when the buddy repeats the human's sentence.
ended_conversation is true when the buddy says goodbye or closes the talk.
language_match is true when the buddy speaks the session language.

A critique quotes a spoken line or a field of the lesson subject. Human speech errors are not scored.
