Title: Spider Brackfast
The waiter brought breakfast on a white plate, the Italian way, and the food was what I swallow. Flies lay in a little olive oil, like a small antipasto, and I took them one by one. Ants were scattered over the warm cornetto the way sugar usually is, and they were good. A beetle had been toasted until it was crisp, dark as espresso, and I ate that too. The cup was only there for the smell. We talked about the street and the bells while I finished the plate. I said I wanted this breakfast again, flies, ants, and the crisp beetle.
