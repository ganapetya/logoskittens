Title: Friendly chat
I stayed with you after the others had gone, and the talk was easy. You laughed at something small, and I kept that sound with me. I told you I was glad you were here, and you let the words stand. Your hand rested near mine on the table. We spoke about the walk home and the light in the window, and the ordinary things felt close. I liked the quiet between our sentences. Before we stood up I said I wanted another evening like this, and you said you did too.
