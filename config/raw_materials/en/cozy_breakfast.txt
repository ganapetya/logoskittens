Title: Cozy Breakfast
The waiter brought breakfast on a white plate, the Italian way, and the morning felt soft and slow. There was a warm cornetto with a little sugar on top, and I broke it in two to smell the butter. A small bowl of berries sat beside it, red and dark blue, and they were sweet. The espresso was dark and hot, and I held the cup with both hands. Sunlight came through the window and lay on the tablecloth. We talked about the street and the bells while I finished the plate. I said I wanted this breakfast again, the cornetto, the berries, and the quiet light.
